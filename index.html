Marketplace insights. Original product ideas.

Product Intelligence Studio is a privately operated research and design development application. It helps evaluate marketplace categories, discover trends, and develop original digital and print-on-demand product concepts using AI-assisted workflows.

What the application does

Marketplace research

Studies publicly available listing information, including titles, prices, descriptions, keywords, and available marketplace indicators.

Opportunity analysis

Organizes research over time to evaluate product categories, demand signals, design conventions, and opportunities for differentiation. Public signals are not represented as verified individual sales totals.

Original design development

Supports AI-assisted ideation, original artwork creation, product mockups, and draft listing preparation with manual review before any publication.

Data use & privacy

The application is intended for private use by its operator, runs locally, and is not offered as a public service. Marketplace data is accessed only through authorized methods and in accordance with applicable API permissions and platform terms. The application does not seek access to other sellers' private sales records.

Research records and drafts are stored locally. When AI functionality is enabled, task-related content may be sent to configured AI service providers for processing under their applicable policies. API credentials are not published on this website. No public user accounts are offered.

Publishing, external account changes, and other consequential actions require operator review and approval. The application is under development; described capabilities may be expanded or revised.
